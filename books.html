The lost Star

Summary: The Lost Star follows a courageous young woman whose quiet life is upended by a mysterious stranger and a secret that could change everything. As she navigates love, betrayal, and the cost of truth, she must decide what — and who — she is willing to risk for the life she wants. A tender, suspenseful romance that slowly reveals how past choices shape our chance for hope.

Shadows of Time

Summary: Shadows of Time is an elegant, sweeping tale about how long-buried memories can upend the present. When an old letter surfaces, it forces two families to confront hidden promises and painful regrets, sending ripples through the people who once loved and those still learning to trust. For readers who love emotional depth and the slow burn of secrets finally spoken.
